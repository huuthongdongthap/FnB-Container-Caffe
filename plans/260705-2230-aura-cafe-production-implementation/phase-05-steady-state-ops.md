## Decision Gates

### Gate 1: Month 2 — Continue or Pivot?
- **Go:** Break-even within reach (2,000+ orders), D30 repeat >40%
- **Pause:** <1,500 orders, negative trajectory → pause zone expansion
- **Kill:** <1,000 orders, no improvement → escalate

### Gate 2: Month 4 — Second Zone?
- **Go:** Break-even sustained 2 months, 200+ Premium subs
- **Wait:** Break-even but flat growth → fix marketing first
- **No:** Still losing money → consolidate, optimize

### Gate 3: Month 12 — Multi-Location?
- **Go:** 4,500+ orders/month, 1.5B revenue, proven ops playbook
- **Wait:** 3,000-4,000 → extend current location optimization
- **No:** <3,000 → diagnose before expanding

