## Contractor Selection Criteria

1. **Experience** with container cafes or modular buildouts
2. **Local** to Đồng Tháp / Cần Thơ / HCMC (logistics)
3. **Portfolio** with photos of previous container projects
4. **Fixed-price contract** — no time-and-materials
5. **Warranty** minimum 1 year structural + 6 months finishes

## Decision: Frame vs Turnkey

| Option | Pros | Cons | Recommendation |
|--------|------|------|----------------|
| **Frame** (buy containers, fitout in-house) | Cheaper 20-30%, more control | Requires project management, longer timeline | If owner has construction experience |
| **Turnkey** (vendor handles everything) | Fixed timeline, single point of contact, warranty | 20-30% premium | **Recommended** — owner is NOT in construction |
