## A. HE THONG MA GIAM GIA (PROMOTIONS)

### A1. Cac loai ma giam gia
| Loai | Mo ta | Vi du |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Phan tram** | Giam theo % hoa don | AURA10 — giam 10% |
| **So tien co dinh** | Giam mot so tien | CHECKIN30K — giam 30k |
| **Mien phi van chuyen** | Giam phi ship | SHIP0 — mien phi ship |
| **Combo** | Giam khi mua nhieu mon | COMBO2 — mua 2 mon giam 20% |

### A2. Tao ma giam gia moi
1. Vao Admin > Promotions (`/admin/promotions`)
2. Bam "Tao Ma Moi"
3. Nhap thong tin:
   - **Ma code:** (vi du: SUMMER2026)
   - **Loai:** Phan tram / So tien
   - **Gia tri:** (10% hoac 20,000 VND)
   - **Giam toi da:** (neu phan tram)
   - **Don toi thieu:** (vi du: 50,000 VND)
   - **So luot su dung:** (vi du: 100)
   - **Thoi gian:** Tu ngay — Den ngay
   - **Trang thai:** Active / Inactive
4. Luu → Ma giam gia kich hoat

### A3. Kiem tra ma giam gia
1. Vao Admin > Promotions
2. Xem danh sach ma dang hoat dong
3. Kiem tra: so luot da su dung, con lai
4. Tat/bat ma khi can

### A4. Ma giam gia hien co (Seed Data)
| Ma | Giam | Toi da | Toi thieu | Han |
|----|------|--------|-----------|-----|
| AURA20 | 20% | 50k | 0 | Khai truong (het) |
| AURA10 | 10% | 30k | 0 | Tuan khai truong (het) |

## B. CHIEN DICH TU DONG (CAMPAIGNS)

### B1. Cac loai chien dich
| Chien dich | Kich hoat | Hanh dong |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Sinh nhat** | Ngay sinh nhat khach | Gui voucher giam X% |
| **Winback** | Khach khong den > 30 ngay | Gui loi moi quay lai |
| **Chao mung** | Khach moi dang ky | Gui voucher chao mung |

### B2. Cau hinh chien dich
1. Vao Admin > Campaigns (`/admin/campaigns`)
2. Chon loai chien dich
3. Cau hinh:
   - **Kich hoat:** Bat/Tat
   - **Kenh gui:** Zalo ZNS / SMS / Email
   - **Thoi gian:** Gui luc may gio, ngay nao
   - **Noi dung:** Template thong bao
4. Luu → Chien dich chay tu dong

### B3. Gui thu cong
1. Vao Admin > Broadcast (`/admin/broadcast`)
2. Chon doi tuong:
   - Tat ca khach hang
   - VIP (Gold/Platinum)
   - Khach moi
   - Sinh nhat thang nay
   - Khach khong den > 30 ngay
3. Nhap noi dung thong bao
4. Xem truoc → Gui
5. He thong gui toi danh sach da chon

## C. KENH MARKETING

### C1. Zalo ZNS
- **Su dung:** Gui thong bao khach hang (voucher, sinh nhat, cashback)
- **Yeu cau:** Zalo OA da duoc duyet
- **Template:** Can duyet truoc khi su dung
- **Chi phi:** ~200-500 VND/tin

### C2. SMS (SpeedSMS)
- **Su dung:** Gui OTP, thong bao quan trong
- **Chi phi:** ~300-500 VND/tin
- **Luu y:** Chi gui khi can thiet (khong spam)

### C3. Email (Resend)
- **Su dung:** Receipt, xac nhan dat cho, newsletter
- **Chi phi:** Free tier 100 email/ngay
- **Luu y:** Email chi gui khi co dia chi email hop le

### C4. Mixpost (Social Media)
- **Su dung:** Dang bai Facebook, Instagram, TikTok
- **Tu dong:** Chay cron job hang ngay
- **Noi dung:** Mon moi, khuyen mai, su kien, highlight

### C5. Telegram (Admin)
- **Su dung:** Thong bao don hang moi, canh bao he thong
- **Chi phi:** Free
- **Luu y:** Chi gui cho admin, khong gui khach

## D. QUY TRINH TAO CHIEN DICH MARKETING

### D1. Chien dich khai truong / su kien lon
1. **Chuan bi (T-7 ngay):**
   - [ ] Thiet ke ma giam gia
   - [ ] Viet noi dung thong bao (Zalo, SMS, Email)
   - [ ] Chuan bi hinh anh/social media
   - [ ] Test ma giam gia tren he thong
2. **Kich hoat (T-1 ngay):**
   - [ ] Bat ma giam gia tren he thong
   - [ ] Gui thong bao Zalo cho tat ca khach
   - [ ] Dang bai social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
   - [ ] Dat banner tai quan
3. **Chay su kien:**
   - [ ] Theo doi don hang, ma giam gia su dung
   - [ ] Xu ly van de (ma loi, khach phan nan)
   - [ ] Cap nhat social media (stories, reels)
4. **Ket thuc:**
   - [ ] Tat ma giam gia
   - [ ] Tong ket: so luot su dung, doanh thu, loi nhuan
   - [ ] Gui cam on khach hang

### D2. Chien dich nho (Hang tuan)
1. Chon mon dac biet (mon moi, mon theo mua)
2. Viet noi dung ngan gon
3. Dang len social media
4. Gui thong bao Zalo (neu co uu dai)
