# SOP 04 — Loyalty & Quan Ly Khach Hang

> **Ap dung:** AURA CAFE Sa Dec
> **Nguoi thuc hien:** Tat ca staff, Manager, Owner
> **He thong:** Loyalty v2 — 4 hang, cashback, diem, referral, sinh nhat

---

## A. HE THONG HANG THANH VIEN

### A1. Bang hang thanh vien

| Hang | Chi tieu tich luy | Cashback | He so diem | Sinh nhat | Han ví |
|------|-------------------|----------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 🥉 **Bronze** (Dong) | 0 — 500k | **3%** | x1.0 | Giam 10% | 90 ngay |
| 🥈 **Silver** (Bac) | 500k — 5M | **5%** | x1.2 | Giam 20% | 120 ngay |
| 🥇 **Gold** (Vang) | 5M — 15M | **7%** | x1.5 | Giam 35% | 180 ngay |
| 💎 **Platinum** (Bach Kim) | > 15M | **10%** | x2.0 | Giam 50% + Qua | Vinh vien |

### A2. Quy tac tich luy
- **Diem =** (Tien sau giam) × he so hang / 1000
- **Cashback** = (Tien mat thuc te) × ty le hang
- **Khong tich luy** tren tien da dung tu ví cashback (no loop)
- **Gioi han** cashback toi da: 50.000 VND/don (100k ngay khai truong)

### A3. Ví cashback
- Tien duoc **cong ngay** sau khi thanh toan thanh cong
- Khach co the su dung toi **50% hoa don** bang ví
- Ví co **han su dung** theo hang (Bronze 90 ngay, v.v.)
- Khi het han — tien ví bi mat (khong hoan)

## B. DANG KY THANH VIEN

### B1. Khach dang ky online
1. Khach vao trang menu → nhap so dien thoai
2. He thong tu dong tao tai khoan thanh vien
3. Khach nhan thong bao chao mung (Zalo ZNS / SMS)
4. Khach duoc hang **Bronze** ngay lap tuc

### B2. Dang ky tai quay (Staff ho tro)
1. Hoi khach: "Anh/ch co muon dang ky thanh vien AURA CAFE khong?"
2. Nhap so dien thoai khach vao he thong
3. Xac nhan thong tin: Ten, email (neu co)
4. He thong tao tai khoan → hang Bronze

### B3. Quy tac dang ky
- **Moi so dien thoai** chi duoc dang ky **1 lan**
- **Khong can mat khau** — dang nhap bang OTP/sdt
- **Khong can email** — email la tuy chon

## C. TICH LUY & SU DUNG CASHBACK

### C1. Tich luy cashback
1. Khach thanh toan don hang (PayOS hoac COD)
2. He thong tu dong tinh cashback:
   - Tien mat thuc te × ty le hang = cashback
   - Gioi han toi da 50k/don
3. Cashback duoc **cong vao ví** ngay lap tuc
4. Khach nhan thong bao: "Ban da nhan X VND cashback!"

### C2. Su dung cashback
1. Khi thanh toan — he thong hien "Su dung ví: X VND"
2. Khach chon su dung → tru toi da 50% hoa don
3. Tien mat khach can tra = Hoa don - Cashback da su dung
4. **Luu y:** Cashback cu duoc su dung truoc (FIFO)

### C3. Tra loi khach ve cashback
- **Khach hoi:** "Cashback la gi?" → "La tien hoa hong quy khach duoc hoan lai sau moi don, co the dung cho don sau."
- **Khach hoi:** "Tai sao toi chi duoc dung 50%?" → "Day la quy dinh de dam bao dong tien on dinh cho quan."
- **Khach hoi:** "Cashback het han khi nao?" → "Tuy hang: Bronze 90 ngay, Silver 120 ngay, Gold 180 ngay, Platinum vinh vien."

## D. TICH LUY & SU DUNG DIEM

### D1. Tich luy diem
- Diem = (Tien sau giam) × he so hang / 1000
- Vi du: Khach Gold mua 100k → Diem = 100k × 1.5 / 1000 = 150 diem
- Diem duoc cong ngay sau thanh toan

### D2. Doi diem lay thuong
1. Khach vao trang Loyalty → Xem "Thuong cua ban"
2. Chon mon muon doi (tra, ca phe, etc.)
3. Kiem tra so du diem du
4. Xac nhan doi → tru diem, tao voucher
5. Voucher hien tren KDS nhu don binh thuong

### D3. Gia tri doi diem
| Mon doi | Diem can | Gia tri (VND) |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Ca phe den | 100 diem | 20,000 |
| Ca phe sua | 125 diem | 25,000 |
| Tra | 150 diem | 30,000 |
| Sinh to | 175 diem | 35,000 |

**Chi doi duoc cac mon tieu chuan** — khong doi mon dac biet/co gia cao.

## E. CHUONG TRINH GIOI THIEU (REFERRAL)

### E1. Co che
- Moi khach thanh vien co **ma gioi thieu rieng**
- Nguoi gioi thi duoc **10.000VND cashback** khi nguoi duoc gioi thieu thanh toan don dau (toi thieu 20k)
- Nguoi duoc gioi thieu **khong nhan** thuong gioi thieu (don gian hoa)
- Chan **tu gioi thieu** qua IP

### E2. Quy trinh
1. Khach vao trang Referral → Xem ma gioi thieu
2. Chia se ma voi ban be
3. Ban be nhap ma khi dang ky / dat hang
4. Khi ban be thanh toan don dau (>= 20k) → nguoi gioi thieu nhan 10k cashback
5. He thong tu dong thong bao ca hai

### E3. Tra loi khach
- **Khach hoi:** "Ma gioi thieu cua toi o dau?" → "Vao trang /referral tren dien thoai, ma cua ban la: XXX"
- **Khach hoi:** "Toi duoc bao nhieu tien?" → "10.000VND cashback khi ban be thanh toan don dau >= 20k."

## F. UU DAI SINH NHAT

### F1. Co che
- He thong tu dong kiem tra ngay sinh nhat khach
- Gui uu dai truoc **7 ngay** sinh nhat
- Uu dai: **Giam 10-50%** tuy hang (Bronze 10%, Platinum 50%)
- Han su dung: **7 ngay** ke tu ngay sinh nhat

### F2. Quy trinh
1. He thong phat hien sinh nhat khach (cron job chay hang ngay)
2. Tao voucher rieng cho khach
3. Gui thong bao: "Chuc mung sinh nhat! Ban duoc giam X% cho don tiep theo."
4. Khach su dung voucher khi dat hang
5. Voucher chi su dung **1 lan**, het han sau 7 ngay

### F3. Tra loi khach
- **Khach hoi:** "Sinh nhat toi co gi?" → "AURA giam X% cho ban trong 7 ngay quanh sinh nhat. Vao /loyalty de nhan voucher."

## G. CHECK-IN THUONG

### G1. Co che
- Khach check-in tai quan → nhan thuong
- **1 lan/thang** moi khach
- Giai doan khai truong: +20k cashback
- Sau khai truong: giam 10% truc tiep

### G2. Quy trinh
1. Khach vao `/checkin` tren dien thoai
2. Chup anh tai quan (bat buoc)
3. Gui yeu cau check-in
4. Staff duyet tren `/admin/checkin-approve`
5. Neu duyet → khach nhan thuong
6. Neu tu choi → thong bao ly do
