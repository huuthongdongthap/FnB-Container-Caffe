# Full menu imagery

## Goal and scope
Extend the approved warm daylight photography style to all 49 menu entries across ten categories. Reuse the six accepted images and generate 43 additional distinct images, matched to product identity. A combined hot/iced or flavor entry uses one representative serving; packaged drink entries use clearly unbranded illustrative packaging.

## Constraints
Use real menu names, IDs and existing prices. Keep custom administrator image URLs. Never reuse coffee images for cocoa, tea images for juice, or hot latte images for frappuccino. No Viva Star marks or lettering. Do not alter source references. Save PNG masters and 1200 × 900 WebP derivatives locally. No deployment or database mutation.

## Acceptance
- 49 of 49 canonical items resolve to existing, distinct product assets.
- Existing fallback menu entries also use the appropriate new photographs; support its yo/yg yogurt ID aliases and grouped smoothie representation without changing product choices.
- A responsive preview shows the full collection with category filtering, names and downloads.
- Inspect every image for subject accuracy, visible branding and complete vessel framing.
- Validate canonical coverage, unknown/custom image preservation, image decoding, menu tests, TypeScript and desktop/mobile previews.
