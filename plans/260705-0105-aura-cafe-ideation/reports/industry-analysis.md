## 1. Vietnam F&B Market Overview (2025-2026)

### 1.1 Market Size & Structure

Vietnam's F&B sector is in a major consolidation phase. Key macro numbers:

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Coffee & tea chain market value (2026) | ~$2 billion USD | DPS Media F&B Report 2026 |
| Coffee chain outlets (2019 vs 2025) | 816 -> 2,067 (2.5x growth) | B&Company / Insight Asia |
| Beverage chain market (2025) | $1.3 billion, 3rd in SE Asia | Momentum Works / VnExpress |
| Consumer Foodservice (2025) | VND 678 trillion, +5% YoY | Euromonitor |
| F&B outlets closed H1 2025 | 50,000+ (7.1% decline) | Industry reports |
| HCMC + Hanoi cafe decline | >11% each | Industry reports |
| Online coffee sales growth | +133% YoY (H1 2025) | DPS Media |

### 1.2 "The Great Cleanup" — Consolidation Dynamics

The 2025 market is described as the "year of purification" (Nguyen Thai Binh, Concepts Academy). Weak operating models are being purged due to:

- **Rising input costs:** Raw materials + CPI for food/dining up 3.69% in H1 2025
- **Rising rents:** Central-district rents up 15% vs 2024 in HCMC and Hanoi
- **Tighter consumer spending:** Post-pandemic caution
- **Unsustainable expansion models:** Rapid open -> rapid close models no longer viable

**Implication for AURA CAFE:** Sa Dec is NOT in a central district, so AURA avoids the 15% rent inflation affecting HCMC/Hanoi. The consolidation also means weaker local competitors in tier-2 cities are closing, leaving room for a quality operator.

### 1.3 Consumer Behavior Shifts (Gen Z Focus)

| Trend | Data Point |
|-------|-----------|
| Visit frequency | 30.4% of consumers visit coffee shops 1-2x/week (+7.8pp) |
| Work-from-cafe (WFC) | 40% choose shops for work purposes |
| Gen Z preferences | Cold coffee, fusion concepts (matcha + coconut juice) |
| Peak spending demographic | 5-10M VND/month income bracket |
| Dine-in share (2025) | 75.22% of foodservice market (delivery plateaus at ~30%) |

**Key insight:** The cafe has evolved from "place to drink coffee" to "third place" — co-working space, content creation studio, social gathering spot. AURA's 5-zone design directly addresses this shift.

## 2. Container Cafe Trend in Vietnam

### 2.1 Market Drivers

The container cafe model is gaining momentum, especially in Southern Vietnam:

| Driver | Impact on AURA CAFE |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Rising real estate costs** | Container model is 40-60% cheaper than brick-and-mortar construction |
| **Flexibility & mobility** | Containers can be relocated, expanded, or reconfigured |
| **Fast deployment** | Weeks vs months for traditional construction |
| **Sustainability appeal** | Repurposed containers align with eco-conscious values |
| **Aesthetic uniqueness** | Industrial-chic attracts younger demographics seeking novelty |
| **Cost savings** | Estimated 360-540M VND for 5 zones vs 800M+ for traditional build |

### 2.2 Competitive Landscape: Alternative Formats

Major chains are already moving to compact, flexible formats:

| Format | Description | Adopted By |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Compact kiosks (<20m2) | Infiltrate office buildings and transit hubs | Phuc Long, Katinat |
| Drive-thru | Motorbike-friendly quick service | Highlands Coffee |
| Takeaway kiosks | Low-cost, high-volume models | Multiple chains |
| Tech-carts | Mobile sales for high-traffic areas | Katinat, Phuc Long |
| **Container cafes** | Full experience zones | **AURA CAFE (first-mover in Sa Dec)** |

### 2.3 Container Cafe — National vs Local Context

**National level:** Container cafes are concentrated in HCMC (District 1, District 3), industrial zones, and tourist areas. Providers like An Gia Phat Container (HCMC/Dong Nai) supply modified containers for F&B use.

**Local level (Dong Thap / Sa Dec):** Zero container cafe competitors. AURA CAFE is the first-mover in the Mekong Delta region for industrial-luxury container concept.

> **First-mover advantage:** No direct container cafe competitor within 50km of Sa Dec.

| NAPAS transactions (9 months 2025) | 8.3+ billion | State Bank of Vietnam |

### 3.2 QR as a Strategic Weapon, Not Just Payments

The industry is shifting from "QR as payment" to "QR as full ordering experience":

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Eliminates paper menu costs** | Digital-first, instant updates |
| **Reduces front-of-house staff** | Lower FOH headcount needed |
| **Increases average order value** | Digital upselling prompts + combo suggestions |
| **Orders go directly to KDS** | Zero communication errors |
| **Data collection** | Track popularity, peak times, preferences |
| **Faster table turns** | <5 min order-to-submit vs 10-15 min with waiter |
| **Zero commission fees** | Bypasses GrabFood/ShopeeFood 20-30% fees |

### 3.3 Chain-Level QR Adoption

| Chain | QR Implementation Level |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Highlands Coffee** | Full: App-based order ahead + QR at store + e-wallet payments |
| **The Coffee House** | Full: App ordering, QR menu, loyalty integration |
| **Katinat** | Full: App ordering (OOD Platform), QR at store, multi-payment |
| **Cong Caphe** | Partial: Accepts QR payments but no dedicated order-ahead app |
| **Local independents** | Mixed: Many still paper menu + cash, some accepting QR payments |

> **AURA CAFE advantage:** QR ordering built from scratch as the PRIMARY ordering channel. No legacy paper-menu dependency. All 30+ features (loyalty, referral, KDS, POS) integrated into one QR flow.
