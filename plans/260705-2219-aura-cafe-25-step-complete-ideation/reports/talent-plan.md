## 1. Executive Summary

AURA CAFE operates a 5-zone container cafe with QR ordering, events, and subscriptions. The business model is **labor-lean by design**: QR ordering eliminates order-takers, KDS automates kitchen routing, and digital payments reduce cash handling. However, 3 revenue streams (F&B + Events + Subscriptions) and 5 distinct zones create complexity that requires intentional staffing.

**Core thesis:** Keep frontline headcount minimal (technology substitutes labor). Invest headroom in:
- Barista quality (product is the differentiator)
- Event coordination (second revenue stream owner)
- Community management (retention + subscription upsell)

**Recommended steady-state team:** 7-9 FTE + 2-3 part-time/casual
**Monthly staff cost (target):** 38-48M VND (within the 30-40M estimate from unit economics)

## 12. Open Questions for Founder

1. **Owner involvement:** Will you (the founder) work full-time in the cafe, or oversee remotely? This changes whether an Assistant Manager is needed immediately.
2. **Events DIY vs hire:** Do you want to run events personally for the first 1-2 months before hiring a coordinator? This saves salary but delays event revenue.
3. **Marketing ownership:** Should marketing be in-house (PT hire) or outsourced to a Sa Dec agency (~5-8M/month)? In-house gives more control at lower cost.
4. **Staff accommodation:** For key hires who may not live in Sa Dec center, is staff housing or transport allowance needed?
5. **Tip pool policy:** All tips shared equally by hours worked, or performance-weighted? Equal is simpler and creates team culture; performance-weighted rewards top talent.
6. **Family/labor law:** Are all staff registered with social insurance (BHXH)? This adds ~21.5% on top of salary but is legally required for FT contracts.
