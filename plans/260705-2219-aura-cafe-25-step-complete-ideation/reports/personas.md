## Persona 1: Thanh — Gioi tre Sa Dec (18-35)

### Demographics
- **Name:** Thanh (nu)
- **Age:** 22, sinh vien nam cuoi / moi ra truong
- **Location:** Trung tam Sa Dec hoac phu can (Lai Vung, Cao Lanh)
- **Income:** 3-8 trieu/thang (part-time / viec van phong entry-level)
- **Social:** Dung Instagram/TikTok 4+ gio/ngay, thuong xuyen check-in
- **Group size:** Di nhom 2-4 nguoi (ban than, crush, hoi sinh vien)

### Pain Points (Noi dau)

| Pain Point | Chi tiet |
|---|---|
| **Khong co noi chup hinh dep** | Cac quan o Sa Dec decor cu, thieu goc so. Anh up len social khong dat luot tuong tac. |
| **Ngai gia cao** | Ngan sach eo hep. Cafe 50K/ly la phai canh tac. Mon gia cao = khong quay lai. |
| **Quan chat / kho tim cho** | Cuoi tuan quan nao cung dong. Khong co khong gian rieng cho nhom ban. |
| **Trai nghiem nham chan** | Menu giong nhau khap noi (ca phe den, bac xiu, sinh to). Thieu mon "sang tao". |

### Triggers (Chat chan — khi nao thi di?)

1. **Thay bai dang "soc nhiet"** — Mot bai TikTok/reel ve AURA CAFE tu mot KOL dia phuong. Anh container, den neon, goc chup "xinh xin". Clip dat 50K+ views -> "Di thu hom nay!"
2. **Ban ru di "khai truong"** — Nhom chat hoi sinh vien: "Mai khai truong AURA container, di khong?" FOMO.
3. **Co dip sinh nhat / ky niem** — Can mot noi chup hinh + an mung nho. AURA co goi Events + check-in corner.
4. **Date lan dau** — Can mot khong gian "dep ma khong ngai." AURA's Noir Cabin (dark + intimate) dat chuan.
5. **Khung gia 15K-35K** — Mot ly matcha latte 29K. Re hon ca Highlands, dep hon gap 3. So sanh nay danh bay ngai gia.

### Dream State (Trang thai mong muon)

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Muon di chup hinh nhung khong co noi dep o Sa Dec" | "Len AURA, chup 1 bo anh tha ho, dang len story duoc 300 view" |
| "Ngai ton tien 100K cho mot buoi cafe" | "Chi ton 45K — 1 ly + 1 banh, van co goc chup sieu dep" |
| "Cac quan dong duc, khong co khong gian rieng" | "Den Jade Counter buoi trua, it nguoi, check-in thoai mai" |
| "Menu nha cham, cafe dau la cafe" | "AURA co mon signature — Matcha Dusk, Espresso Tonic — giong nhu o Sai Gon" |

### AURA Touchpoints
- **First contact:** TikTok/Instagram Reel -> Landing page (auraspace.cafe)
- **On arrival:** QR ordering -> chon Sky Deck / Jade Counter
- **Engagement:** Check-in corner -> chup hinh -> tag AURA -> nhan loyalty points
- **Retention:** Referral program -> dan ban -> tich diem -> Pre-order qua Subscription

## Persona 2: Chi Hien — Dan van phong (25-40)

### Demographics
- **Name:** Chi Hien
- **Age:** 31, quan ly nhan su / ke toan
- **Location:** Trung tam Sa Dec, noi lam viec gan 39 Nguyen Tat Thanh
- **Income:** 10-20 trieu/thang, co kha nang chi tieu
- **Schedule:** Thu 2-7 la viec, CN nghi. Gio hanh chinh + tang ca optional.
- **Style:** Mac lich su, can wifi manh, cho yeu tinh, ngai on ao

### Pain Points (Noi dau)

| Pain Point | Chi tiet |
|---|---|
| **Thieu khong gian lam viec tinh** | Cafe hien tai o Sa Dec qua on ao, nhac xo, ban nho khong de laptop. |
| **Can cho hop offline / gap khach** | Nha hang trang trong => dat tien. Quan coc => mat mat. Thieu khong gian "chat luong trung binh-cao." |
| **Wifi yeu, it o dien** | Laptop het pin, wifi lag, khong the lam viec ca buoi. |
| **Ngai di mot minh** | Mot minh vao quan dong => ngai. Can mot noi "thuoc ve minh," nhan vien nho mat, co goc yeu tinh. |

### Triggers (Chat chan — khi nao thi di?)

1. **Co cuoc hop ngoai gio** — Can gap khach hang / dong nghiep buoi sang som hay cuoi tuan. AURA's Aura Lounge (quiet, private).
2. **Can "reset" giua tuan** — Thu 4 metam. Can mot ly cafe + khong gian yen tinh de nap nang luong.
3. **Khach hang ru gap** — "Hen o AURA di, quan nay dep, co wifi, dat cho truoc duoc."
4. **Thay quang cao "Co-Working Package"** — Combo cafe + wifi manh + o dien ca ngay 49K. Qua tien loi.
5. **Ngay cuoi tuan muon "thoat khoi nha"** — CN khong muon o nha. Ra AURA, ngoi Sky Deck, gio laptop ra lam 2 tieng -> ve.

### Dream State (Trang thai mong muon)

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Khong co noi nao yeu tinh de lam viec gan van phong" | "Den Sky Deck trua, co wifi 100MB, o dien, 1 ly batch brew 35K, lam ca buoi chieu" |
| "Gap khach hang phai dat nha hang Phoi Pho, min 300K" | "Hen o Aura Lounge, goi Premium tea 49K, khach thich, minh dot ton" |
| "Cuoi tuan khong biet di dau, o nha lai buon" | "AURA co Evening Jazz thu Bay: vua cafe vua nhac, 1 minh cung khong ngai" |
| "Muon tich diem nhung khong quan nao co" | "AURA Loyalty tang Voucher sau 5 ly, subscription tiet kiem 20%" |

### AURA Touchpoints
- **First contact:** Google Maps search "cafe lam viec Sa Dec" -> doc review -> den thu
- **On arrival:** Pre-order (Subscription) -> check-in -> chon Aura Lounge / VIP Steel Nest
- **Engagement:** Laptop corner + wifi + o dien -> lam viec -> loyalty points
- **Retention:** Email newsletter -> event notification -> Subscription auto-refill

## Persona 3: Anh Vu — Khach du lich (22-50)

### Demographics
- **Name:** Anh Vu
- **Age:** 34, ky su / freelancer hoac 45, gia dinh co con nho
- **Location:** TP. Ho Chi Minh (or Can Tho, My Tho) -> Sa Dec (di choi cuoi tuan)
- **Income:** 15-40 trieu/thang, du kich chi tieu cho chuyen di
- **Travel style:** Di xe may / xe hoi 2-4 tieng. "Google Maps qua diem den."
- **Purpose:** Trai nghiem, chup anh, an uong, mua dac san

### Pain Points (Noi dau)

| Pain Point | Chi tiet |
|---|---|
| **Ngai di cho xa, khong biet Sa Dec co gi** | Thanh pho nho, chua co brand entertainment. "Len Sa Dec co gi de choi?" |
| **Thieu diem den "Instagrammable"** | Lang hoa, chua, cho. Khong co noi "xuong xe cafe 20 phut" ma lay social content. |
| **Lo do uong / service quality** | Quan tinh chat luong khong dong deu. Den roi that vong. |
| **Khong co "trai nghiem ban dem"** | Homestay xong... khong biet di dau buoi toi. Sa Dec toi bat den som. |

### Triggers (Chat chan — khi nao thi di?)

1. **Thay AURA tren Google Maps (+ reviews, + photos)** — "Container industrial-luxury o Sa Dec? Thu xem."
2. **Chuyen phuot cuoi tuan** — Di xe may tu Sai Gon -> Cao Lanh -> Sa Dec. Can 1 diem dung chan "xung dang check-in."
3. **Ban be / travel blogger da check-in** — "Hom truoc thay Huy (travel blogger) check-in AURA, 5 khong gian. Cf location di."
4. **Click vao auraspace.cafe** — Landing page hien thi container + den neon + ban cong. "Nhin muon di ngay."
5. **Muon tim "dat san pham doc quyen"** — Ao thun AURA, bean bags, coffee kit. Mua ve lam ky niem.

### Dream State (Trang thai mong muon)

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Sa Dec thuong khong co diem nao 'wow' đe check-in" | "AURA co 5 Studio Zones: Sky Deck chup hoang hon, Noir Cabin chup cam xuc — du content cho ca tuan" |
| "Lo service ko tot, vao roi muon di ra" | "Order bang QR, 5 phut co tra. Nhan vien chat luong. Muon o lai thu them" |
| "Sa Dec 8h toi la vang, khong biet lam gi" | "AURA co Nocturnal Session toi Thu 7 — jazz, den neon, chill — o choi den 11h" |
| "Muon mua qua luu niem dac biet" | "AURA merch: tui tote, sticker, coffee drip bag. Mua 5 set ve tang team" |

### AURA Touchpoints
- **First contact:** Google Maps / Instagram / Travel blog -> landing page (auraspace.cafe)
- **On arrival:** Check-in -> chon khu VIP (Sky Deck buoi hoang hon / Aura Lounge ban dem)
- **Engagement:** Chup anh 5 khong gian -> dang story -> mua merch

## Key Takeaways for Marketing

1. **Content nan:** Chup video 5 khong gian (1: ngay, 2: hoang hon, 3: toi) -> TikTok/Reels. Moi layout cho moi persona.
2. **Pricing packaging:**
   - Gioi tre: Combo "1 ly + 1 banh" 45K
   - Van phong: "Co-working Day Pass" 49K (cafe + wifi + o dien)
   - Du lich: "Sunset Package" 79K (signature drink + khu VIP Sky Deck)
3. **Timing:** Day-part menu — sang cho van phong, chieu cho gioi tre, toi cho du lich + events
4. **Referral:** "Dan ban AURA, tich diem doi vo" — nhom gioi tre la driver chinh cua referral loop
