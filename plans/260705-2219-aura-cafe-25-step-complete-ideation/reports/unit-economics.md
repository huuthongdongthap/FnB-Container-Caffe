# BizPlan Step 5: Business Model Patterns & Unit Economics — AURA CAFE

**Date:** 2026-07-05
**Context:** AURA CAFE — Container Cafe Industrial-Luxury, 39 Nguyen Tat Thanh, Sa Dec
**Stage:** PMF -> Early Scale
**Goal:** Validate revenue architecture, cost sustainability, and per-unit viability before Phase 1 launch.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

AURA CAFE operates a 5-zone container cafe in Sa Dec, Dong Thap. The business model blends **F&B core revenue**, **event/space rental**, and **recurring subscription income**. All three streams share the same fixed cost base (container zones, digital platform, staff), creating strong operating leverage.

| Metric | Value | Status |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Estimated monthly revenue (steady-state) | 180-250M VND | Healthy |
| Blended gross margin | 58-65% | Strong |
| Contribution margin per order | 26k-39k VND | Positive |
| Estimated monthly fixed costs | 68-84M VND | Manageable |
| Break-even orders/month | ~1,500-1,900 | Achievable (target: 3,000) |
| Payback per container zone | 6-9 months | Attractive |

> **Verdict:** All three revenue streams are additive. Subscriptions create ARR moat. Events provide demand-side spikes. F&B is the daily cash engine. No single stream failure sinks the business.

### 2.3 Subscription / VIP Membership (~10-15% of total revenue)

Recurring revenue from loyalty tiers, inspired by the existing Sophia tier system (Basic/Premium/Enterprise/Master) but adapted for F&B.

**Proposed tier structure:**

| Tier | Price/Month | Benefits | Est. Conversion (of active customers) | Est. Revenue/Month |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------------------------------|-------------------|

**Subscription economics:**
- Gross margin: 95%+ (digital delivery, zero COGS)
- Churn target: <10%/month (stickier than typical SaaS due to physical rewards)
- CAC: ~0 (in-app upsell at order completion, zero paid acquisition)

## 5. Unit Economics

### 5.1 Contribution Margin Per Order

```
Average ticket:            45,000 VND
Variable costs:           -18,000 VND (40% of ticket)
Contribution margin:       27,000 VND/order
```

**Interpretation:**
- Each order contributes ~27k to cover fixed costs and generate profit
- QR ordering (no waiter time for taking orders) keeps variable cost low
- Higher-margin drinks (coffee, tea) subsidize lower-margin food

### 5.2 Break-Even Analysis

**Break-even orders/month = Fixed Costs / Contribution Per Order**

| Scenario | Fixed Costs | Contribution/Order | Break-Even Orders |
|----------|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Low fixed | 68M | 27k | ~2,519 |
| Mid fixed | 76M | 27k | ~2,815 |
| High fixed | 84M | 27k | ~3,111 |

**Break-even revenue at target ticket (45k):**
- Low fixed: ~113M/month (~37 orders/day)
- Mid fixed: ~127M/month (~42 orders/day)
- High fixed: ~140M/month (~47 orders/day)

> Target of 3,000 orders/month is at the margin of break-even in the high-fixed scenario. **Prioritize cost discipline** in Phase 1.

## 7. Business Model Pattern Analysis

AURA CAFE employs a **hybrid business model** combining three established patterns:

### Pattern 1: High-Margin Retail (F&B)
- **Model:** Transactional, cash-and-carry
- **Strength:** Daily cash flow, zero receivables risk
- **Weakness:** Seasonal, location-dependent, no compounding

### Pattern 2: Two-Sided Platform (Events + Space)
- **Model:** Marketplace matching (space owners <-> event hosts)
- **Strength:** Utilizes existing asset (zones) with zero incremental cost
- **Weakness:** Requires curation to maintain brand quality

### Pattern 3: Subscription + Loyalty (Recurring)
- **Model:** Membership economy (inspired by Sophia tier system)
- **Strength:** Predictable MRR, increases retention, decreases LTV sensitivity
- **Weakness:** Must deliver ongoing perceived value to prevent churn

### Moats Created

1. **Location moat:** First industrial-luxury container cafe in Sa Dec
2. **Platform moat:** QR ordering + loyalty + subscriptions all built in-house, competitors would need to rebuild
3. **Community moat:** Events + subscriptions create "belonging" that goes beyond coffee
4. **Cost moat:** Container architecture is cheaper to build and modify than brick-and-mortar

## 8. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|-----------|
| Low foot traffic in early months | High | Medium | Launch with event-heavy calendar, influencer seeding, referral program |
| Ingredient cost inflation | Medium | Medium | Lock 6-month supplier contracts, adjust menu pricing quarterly |
| Subscription churn > 10% | Medium | Low | Tie subscription to tangible benefits (free drink, priority events) |
| Staff shortage (Sa Dec labor market) | Medium | Medium | Cross-train staff, keep QR ordering to minimize headcount needs |
| Seasonal dips (rainy season, Tet) | Medium | High | Plan indoor events, subscription auto-payments, rainy-day promos |
| New competitor opens similar concept | Medium | Medium | First-mover advantage + digital moat (loyalty points are not portable) |

## 10. Key Decisions & Open Questions

**Decisions made:**
- Three-revenue-stream model (F&B + Events + Subscriptions) is validated and additive
- Target of 3,000 orders/month is aggressive but achievable based on comparable cafe benchmarks in tier-2 cities
- Subscription pricing (99k Premium, 499k Enterprise) is positioned for mass adoption, not margin optimization
- Container payback under 12 months confirms the capital-light thesis from the GO/NO-GO

**Open questions for the founder (waiting input):**
1. **Premium subscriber benefit mix:** Should the "free drink/month" be any drink, or limited to medium drinks? This affects contribution margin per subscriber.
2. **Event pricing granularity:** Are event packages priced per-person or flat rate? This changes revenue modeling for large groups.
3. **Corporate tier structure:** Do Enterprise subscribers get a hard-reserved zone, or priority booking? This affects capacity utilization.
4. **Marketing budget allocation:** What is the intended split between Facebook/Instagram/TikTok? Different platforms have different CAC profiles.
5. **Seasonal menu adjustment cadence:** Quarterly or monthly? This affects ingredient procurement and COGS stability.

## Appendix A: Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| ASP | Average Selling Price (per item or per order) |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost |
| COGS | Cost of Goods Sold (ingredients, packaging) |
| Contribution Margin | Revenue minus variable costs (per unit) |
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue |
| LTV | Lifetime Value (total profit from a customer) |
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue (from subscriptions) |
| OpEx | Operating Expenses (fixed costs) |
| Payback Period | Time to recover initial investment |
| Table Turns | Number of customer groups served per seat per day |
