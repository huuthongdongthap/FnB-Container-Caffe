## 1. Target Client Profile / Khach Hang Muc Tieu

| Criteria / Tieu Chi | Description / Mo Ta |
|---|---|
| **Loai hinh / Type** | Cafe, tea house, juice bar, quan an nhe doc lap / Independent F&B (coffee, tea, juice, light food) |
| **Hien tai / Currently** | Dang dung giay to, Excel, hoac POS cu / Using paper, Excel, or old POS |
| **Quy mo / Size** | Co 10 ban tro len de QR ordering co gia tri / 10+ seats so QR ordering provides value |
| **Chu quan / Owner** | Ham cong nghe, san sang thu cai moi / Tech-curious, open to trying new things |
| **Vi tri / Location** | TP.HCM, Binh Duong, Dong Nai, Long An (gan de setup truc tiep) / HCMC or nearby for on-site setup |
| **Van de / Pain point** | Nhan vien ban sai mon, cham, thieu nhan luc / Wrong orders, slow service, understaffed |
| **Tiem nang / Potential** | San long tra TRAI BANH AM THUC (co cau chuyen, co guong mat that) / Willing to be a CASE STUDY (name, face, story) |

### Where to Find Them / Tim O Dau

- **Facebook groups:** Hoi quan cafe TP.HCM, Kinh nghiem kinh doanh cafe, Doanh nhan F&B Viet Nam
- **Zalo groups:** Groups for F&B owners / equipment suppliers
- **Google Maps:** Search "cafe gan day" in target districts, visit in person
- **Walk-in:** Di dao truc tiep (Go direct) -- District 1, 3, 5, 7, Binh Thanh, Go Vap, Phu Nhuan
- **Supplier referrals:** Ban hang thiet bi cafe, cafe sup, may xay -- ho biet quan nao moi mo / Equipment and ingredient suppliers know who just opened

### What to Look For / Dau Hieu Nhan Biet

- Menu bang giay, dan bang tay / Hand-written paper menu
- Nhan vien chay ban vo vang / Staff running frantically
- Ban chat dong / Tables crowded on weekends
- Khach goi sua don / Customers changing orders after placing
- Khong co website hoac fanpage cup nhat / No website or outdated social media

## 2. Outreach Scripts / Mau Tin Nhan

### Template 1: Initial Contact / Lan Dau Nhan Tin

**Target:** Zalo or Facebook Messenger
**Goal:** Get their attention, no pressure / Gay chu y, khong gay ap luc

> **Vietnamese:**
> Anh/chi oi, em ben AURA CAFE — ben minh co he thong goi mon bang QR code tren ban. Khach chi can quet QR, goi mon bang dien thoai, khoi can goi nhan vien.
>
> Ben minh dang tim 2 quan dau tien de tang thang dau mien phi (lam case study). Anh/chi co ranh chut xiu khong de em gioi thieu nhanh nhe?

> **English (notes for yourself):**
> Hi [owner], I'm from AURA CAFE. We have a QR ordering system -- customers scan the code at the table and order from their phone. No need to call staff.
> We're looking for the first 2 cafes to offer a FREE first month (for case studies). Got a minute for a quick intro?

---

### Template 2: Follow-Up / Gui Lai Sau 3 Ngay

**Send if no reply after 3 days.**

> **Vietnamese:**
> Chao anh/chi lai a. Em chi muon gui them 1 anh minh hoa thoi a — he thong cua minh don gian lam, khach quet QR la dat mon duoc ngay. Nhan vien chi viec boi ban, khong can chay di ghi chep nua.
>
> Anh/chi co muon xem thu bang that khong? Neu khong tien thi khong sao dau a.

> **English (notes):**
> Hi again [owner]. Just sharing a screenshot -- the system is super simple. Customer scans QR, orders, staff just serves the table.
> Want to see a live demo? No pressure at all.

---

### Template 3: Meeting Close / Keu Goi Hen Gap

**When they show interest or ask "bao nhieu tien":**

> **Vietnamese:**
> Da, cam on anh/chi da quan tam!
>
> Neu anh/chi co ranh, em sang quan gap truc tiep chung ta nhe. Khoang 30 phut thoi a:
> - Em demo he thong chay that bang dien thoai minh
> - Xem quan cua anh/chi co bao nhieu ban
> - Neu ok thi setup luon trong tuan
>
> Sang mai 10h hay 15h chieu anh/chi ok hon a?

> **English (notes):**
> Thanks for your interest! Can I drop by for 30 min?
> - Live demo on my phone
> - Count your tables
> - Set up this week if you like it
> Tomorrow 10am or 3pm?

## 4. Pricing Conversation Script / Cach Trao Doi Gia

### The Structure / Cau Truc

```
Step 1: Show value first ("khach tu goi mon, nhan vien boi ban")
Step 2: Then mention price ("phi setup 15-30tr mot lan, phi duy tri 2-5tr/thang")
Step 3: Always offer a pilot ("thang dau mien phi cho 2 khach hang dau tien")
```

### Full Script / Noi Dung

> **Vietnamese:**
> Day la bang gia tham khao cua minh a:

| Item / Muc | Price / Gia | Notes / Ghi Chu |
|---|---|---|
| **Phi thiet lap / Setup fee** | 15 - 30 trieu VND (one-time / 1 lan) | Includes: cài đặt toàn bộ hệ thống, tạo menu, in QR, hướng dẫn nhân viên |
| **Phi duy tri / Monthly support** | 2 - 5 trieu VND / thang | Includes: cap nhat menu, bao tri, ho tro Zalo nhanh, cap nhat tinh nang |

### What's Included / Bao Gom

**Setup Fee (15-30M VND) / Phi Thiet Lap Bao Gom:**
- Cai dat he thong website quan ly rieng (System setup with your own management website)
- Tao menu dien tu: danh muc, mon, gia, hinh anh (Digital menu: categories, items, prices, photos)
- In ma QR cho tung ban (Print QR codes for each table)
- Tao tai khoan admin + nhan vien (Admin + staff accounts)
- Training nhan vien trong 1 buoi (Staff training session -- 1 session)
- Training chu quan trong 1 buoi (Owner training session -- 1 session)

**Monthly Fee (2-5M VND) / Phi Duy Tri Bao Gom:**
- Cap nhat menu khi co mon moi (Menu updates when new items are added)
- Bao tri he thong (System maintenance)
- Ho tro Zalo trong gio hanh chinh (Zalo support during business hours)
- Backup du lieu (Data backup)
- Cap nhat tinh nang moi (New feature updates)

### Pilot Offer / Uu Dai Dau Tien

> **For the first 2 clients only:**
> - Free first month (gia tri 2-5 trieu) / Mien phi thang dau (value 2-5M VND)
> - Setup fee giam 50% (chi 7.5-15 trieu) / Setup fee reduced 50%
>
> **Dieu kien / Conditions:**
> - Cho phep chup hinh, quay video de lam case study (Allow photos/video for case study)
> - Tham gia phong van 30 phut sau 1 thang (Participate in a 30-min interview after 1 month)
> - Gioi thieu "Anh/chi dung AURA CAFE" khi co ai hoi (Mention you use AURA CAFE when asked)

## 5. Objection Handling / Cach Phan Hoi Khi Khach Tu Choi

### Objection 1: "Dao nay kho khan, khong co tien" / "Times are tough, no money"

> **Response / Phan hoi:**
> Em hieu a. Em co the de nghi phuong an tra gop:
> - Tra truoc 50% phi setup (7-15 trieu), con lai tra trong 3 thang
> - Hoac dung goi Pilot: thang dau mien phi, chi tra phi setup
> - Neu 1 thang khong thay gia tri thi minh dung lai, khong sao ca

> Em hieu a. (I understand.)
> Options:
> - Pay 50% setup upfront, spread the rest over 3 months
> - Or use the Pilot: first month free, pay only setup fee
> - If after 1 month you don't see value, we stop -- no hard feelings

---

### Objection 2: "He thong nay phuc tap khong?" / "Is the system complicated?"

> **Response / Phan hoi:**
> Don gian lam anh/chi oi. Nhin day a (show admin-manual.md):
> - Chu quan: dang nhap, xem bao cao, xem don hang
> - Nhan vien: chi can boi ban + danh dau don hoan thanh
> - Khach: quet QR, chon mon, dat hang
>
> Cai dat xong, minh training 1 buoi la nhan vien chay duoc ngay. Neu quen cung co Zalo ho tro lien tuc.

> It's very simple. (Show admin-manual.md)
> - Owner: login, view reports, view orders
> - Staff: just serve tables + mark orders done
> - Customer: scan QR, pick items, order
>
> After setup, one training session and staff can handle it. If confused, Zalo support is instant.

---

### Objection 3: "Toi can POS vat ly" / "I need a physical POS"

> **Response / Phan hoi:**
> Da, he thong cua minh co the THAY THE POS cho hau het cac truong hop:
> - Khach goi mon bang QR -> he thong tu dong bao bep (KDS) va bao thu ngan
> - Khach thanh toan: tinh tien truc tiep tren dien thoai hoac ra quay
> - Nhan vien co the dung dien thoai hoac may tinh bang de xu ly don hang
>
> Neu van can POS vat ly de in hoa don, minh co the ho tro kem POS mini. Nhung 90% quan dung QR la du roi.

> Our system REPLACES POS for most cases:
> - Customer orders via QR -> automatically notifies kitchen (KDS) and cashier
> - Payment: settle on phone or at counter
> - Staff use phone or tablet to process orders
>
> If you still need a physical POS for receipts, we can support a mini POS too. But 90% of cafes only need QR.

---

### Objection 4: "Neu khong dung duoc thi sao?" / "What if it stops working?"

> **Response / Phan hoi:**
> Yen tam nhe anh/chi:
> - He thong chay tren web, khong can cai dat gi -- neu co van de, anh/chi mo trinh duyet khac la duoc
> - Truong hop mat mang: menu giay du phong van co san (chung toi in cho anh/chi ngay tu dau)
> - Neu anh/chi khong tiep tuc dung: he thong van chay binh thuong, khong bi khoa lai
> - Khong co rang buoc: dung thu, khong thich thi thoi, khong mat gi

> Don't worry:
> - It runs on the web, no installation needed -- if there's an issue, just open another browser
> - If internet goes down: backup paper menu is ready (we print it during setup)
> - If you stop paying: system keeps working as-is, no lock-out
> - No lock-in: try it, don't like it, stop -- nothing lost

---

### Objection 5: "Toi can gap" / "Phone call -- I need it NOW"

> **Response / Phan hoi:**
> Neu khach hang goi dien thay vi nhan tin:
>
> 1. **Lang nghe** truoc (2-3 phut) -- de ho noi het ly do goi (Listen first -- let them talk)
> 2. **Xac nhan van de** -- "Da, em hieu roi a" (Acknowledge the problem)
> 3. **De xuat demo** -- "Em sang gap duoc khong a? Khoang 30p la xong" (Propose a meeting)
> 4. **Lay Zalo** -- "Cho em xin so Zalo de gui hinh anh va thong tin them nhe" (Get their Zalo for follow-up)
>
> **Tuyet doi khong ep ban tren dien thoai.** Muc tieu cua cuoc goi chi la dat lich gap. Khong ban hang tren dien thoai.
> **NEVER try to close on the phone.** The goal of the call is only to schedule a meeting.
