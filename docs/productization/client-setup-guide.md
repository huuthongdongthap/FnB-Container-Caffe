# Huong Dan Cai Dat / Client Setup Guide

> **AURA CAFE** -- He thong quan ly quan ca phe toan dien / Complete cafe management system

---

Chao mung ban den voi **AURA CAFE**! (Welcome to **AURA CAFE**!)

Day la he thong quan ly quan ca phe toan dien, duoc thiet ke danh rieng cho cac quan ca phe, tra sua, va cua hang an uong nho. (This is a complete cafe management system, designed specifically for cafes, tea shops, and small F&B businesses.)

Voi AURA CAFE, ban co the: (With AURA CAFE, you can:)

-   Quan ly menu va mon an 🍽️ (Manage your menu and items)
-   Khach hang tu goi mon bang QR tren ban 📱 (Customers order via QR codes at the table)
-   Xem bao cao doanh thu moi ngay 📊 (View daily sales reports)
-   Quan ly don hang tu xa 🌐 (Manage orders remotely)

**Khong can kien thuc cong nghe!** Chung toi lo het phan ky thuat. (NO tech knowledge needed! We handle everything technical.)

## 3. Quy Trinh Cai Dat / Setup Process 🔄

Chi **4 buoc** don gian, ban se co he thong chay ngay! (Just **4 simple steps** to get your system running!)

### Buoc 1: Ban Cung Cap Thong Tin (Step 1: You Provide Info) ⏱️ ~30 phut

Gui cho chung toi cac thong tin o muc **Chuan Bi** phia tren qua Zalo hoac email. (Send us the information from the **Prerequisites** section above via Zalo or email.)

> Chung toi se huong dan ban tung buoc! (We will guide you step by step!)

### Buoc 2: Doi Ngu AURA Cai Dat He Thong (Step 2: AURA Team Sets Up the System) ⏱️ ~2-4 gio

Sau khi nhan du thong tin, chung toi se: (After receiving your info, we will:)

-   Tao website quan ly rieng cho quan ban (Create your own management website)
-   Cai dat menu va mon an (Set up menu and items)
-   Tao tai khoan admin cho ban (Create your admin account)
-   Tao ma QR cho ban (Generate table QR codes)

Ban chi can ngoi cho, chung toi lo het! (Just sit back, we handle it all!)

### Buoc 3: Nhan Duong Dan Va Tai Khoan (Step 3: Receive URLs and Login) 🎉

Chung toi se gui cho ban qua Zalo hoac email: (We will send you via Zalo or email:)

-   **Duong dan quan ly** (Management URL): `your-cafe.auraspace.cafe/admin`
-   **Tai khoan** (Account): email + mat khau (email + password)
-   **File QR** cho cac ban (QR code files for your tables)

### Buoc 4: Dang Nhap Lan Dau (Step 4: First Login) 🔑

1.  Mo trinh duyet web (Chrome, Safari, Coc Coc...) tren dien thoai hoac may tinh (Open a web browser on your phone or computer)
2.  Nhap duong dan quan ly duoc gui (Enter the management URL you received)
3.  Nhap email va mat khau (Enter your email and password)
4.  Nhan "Dang Nhap" (Click "Login")

> **Man hinh quan ly se hien ra!** Ban da san sang quan ly quan! (The management dashboard will appear! You are ready to manage!)

### Buoc 5: Them Mon Vao Menu (Step 5: Add Menu Items) 🍕

Sau khi dang nhap, ban co the tu them mon an: (After logging in, you can add items yourself:)

1.  Vao muc **"Quan Ly Menu" / "Menu Management"**
2.  Nhan **"Them Mon" / "Add Item"** ➕
3.  Nhap ten mon, gia, va tai hinh anh (Enter item name, price, and upload photo)
4.  Nhan **"Luu" / "Save"** ✅

> Muon sua hay xoa? Chi can nhan nut **Sua** hoac **Xoa** ben canh moi mon! (Want to edit or delete? Just click the Edit or Delete button next to each item!)

### Buoc 6: Kiem Tra QR Ordering (Step 6: Test QR Ordering) 📱

1.  In ma QR cua ban (Print your table QR codes)
2.  Dan len ban an (Stick them on the tables)
3.  Dung dien thoai cua ban quet thu ma QR (Use your phone to scan the QR code)
4.  Chon mot mon va dat hang thu (Pick an item and place a test order)
5.  Kiem tra xem don hang co hien tren man hinh quan ly khong (Check if the order appears on the management screen)

> **Neu thay don hang => THANH CONG!** (If you see the order => SUCCESS!) 🎉
