## B. CHIEN DICH TU DONG (CAMPAIGNS)

### Loai chien dich
| Chien dich | Kich hoat | Hanh dong |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Sinh nhat** | Ngay sinh nhat | Gui voucher giam X% |
| **Winback** | Khach > 30 ngay khong den | Gui loi moi quay lai |
| **Chao mung** | Khach moi dang ky | Gui voucher chao mung |

### Cau hinh
1. Vao Admin > Campaigns (`/admin/campaigns`)
2. Chon loai chien dich
3. Cau hinh: Kich hoat, Kenh gui, Thoi gian, Noi dung
4. Luu → Chay tu dong

### Gui thu cong
1. Vao Admin > Broadcast (`/admin/broadcast`)
2. Chon doi tuong: Tat ca / VIP / Moi / Sinh nhat / Khong den > 30 ngay
3. Nhap noi dung → Xem truoc → Gui

## C. KENH MARKETING

### Zalo ZNS
- Gui thong bao: voucher, sinh nhat, cashback
- Yeu cau: Zalo OA duoc duyet
- Chi phi: ~200-500 VND/tin

### SMS (SpeedSMS)
- Gui OTP, thong bao quan trong
- Chi phi: ~300-500 VND/tin
- Chi gui khi can thiet

### Email (Resend)
- Receipt, xac nhan dat cho, newsletter
- Chi phi: Free tier 100 email/ngay

### Mixpost (Social Media)
- Dang bai Facebook, Instagram, TikTok
- Tu dong cron job hang ngay
- Noi dung: Mon moi, khuyen mai, su kien

### Telegram (Admin)
- Thong bao don hang moi, canh bao he thong
- Chi gui cho admin
