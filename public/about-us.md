# Câu chuyện AURA CAFE — Kiến trúc Container Bản Địa

Canonical: https://auraspace.cafe/about-us
Last updated: 2026-11-19

Founded in 2018 in Sa Đéc, Đồng Tháp, AURA CAFE transforms 3 recycled steel shipping containers with frosted tempered glass, natural oak, and greenery into 5 inviting spaces for locals and travelers.
