## Risk Categories

### Technical Risks

Database outages, scalability limits, integration failures, technology obsolescence.

**Key mitigations:**
- Redundancy and failover
- Capacity planning
- Technology diversification
- Regular backups

---

### Business Risks

Market changes, competition, funding issues, customer acquisition costs.

**Key mitigations:**
- Diversified revenue streams
- Cash reserve policy
- Market research
- Customer retention programs

---

### Operational Risks

Staffing, process failures, vendor dependency, compliance violations.

**Key mitigations:**
- Cross-training
- SOP documentation
- Vendor redundancy
- Compliance audits

---

### Security Risks

Data breaches, authentication bypass, DDoS, insider threats.

**Key mitigations:**
- Security scanning
- Penetration testing
- Principle of least privilege
- Incident response plan

---

### Compliance Risks

Regulatory changes, audit failures, licensing issues.

**Key mitigations:**
- Legal counsel
- Compliance monitoring
- Documentation
- Regular audits
